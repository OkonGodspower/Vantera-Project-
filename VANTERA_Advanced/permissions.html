Security

Give each role only the access it needs.

A permission model for school administrators, educators, learners, guardians, business owners, managers and staff.

Role Typical permitted scope Boundary
School admin School membership, classes, authorised reports and administration That school only
Educator Assigned classes, learners, attendance, assignments and grades Assigned classes / school policy
Student Own learning records and approved class content Own account and memberships
Guardian Approved learner summaries and school communications Linked learners only
Business owner Business settings, finance and staff permissions That business workspace
Business staff Tasks and records granted by their assigned role Least-privilege access
This is a design reference, not an enforcement layer. Every permission must be checked server-side for every request; hiding a button is not security.
